# HS302 — Universal Human Values — PLAN

**Semester:** 3 · **Credits:** 2 · **Level:** Intermediate
**Theory:** 30 hrs · **Workshop:** 15 hrs · **Tools:** Reflection journals, group discussions, case studies

## Syllabus

### Unit 1 — Introduction to Value Education (6 lectures)
1. What value education is — and what it is not
2. The need for value education in the 21st century
3. Self-exploration — the process of knowing yourself
4. Natural acceptance vs conditioned assumptions
5. Basic human aspirations — continuous happiness, prosperity
6. Understanding the difference between happiness and pleasure

### Unit 2 — Harmony in the Human Being (6 lectures)
1. The self (I) and the body — two distinct entities
2. Needs of the self vs needs of the body
3. The activities of the self — desire, thought, expectation
4. The activities of the body — eating, moving, breathing
5. Understanding the conscious self — self-awareness
6. Harmony within — the foundation of all other harmony

### Unit 3 — Harmony in the Family and Society (6 lectures)
1. Relationships — the nine core relationships
2. Trust (vishwas) — the foundational value
3. Respect (samman) — right evaluation
4. Affection (sneha) and care (mamata)
5. Guidance and gratitude
6. Harmony in society — family as the basic unit

### Unit 4 — Harmony in Nature and Existence (6 lectures)
1. The four orders of nature — material, pranic, animal, human
2. Interconnectedness and mutual fulfilment
3. The problem of pollution and resource depletion
4. Coexistence — the fundamental law of nature
5. Sustainable lifestyles as value-driven choices
6. The role of technology in maintaining harmony

### Unit 5 — Professional Ethics in Practice (6 lectures)
1. Values in the workplace — honesty, integrity, responsibility
2. Competence as an ethical obligation
3. The student's role — learning as a value
4. Ethical decision-making frameworks
5. Case studies from the tech industry
6. Personal value commitment — a written plan

## Practical Labs (15)

1. Self-reflection journal — first entry, "who am I?"
2. List your desires vs needs — distinguish them
3. Daily practice — 10 minutes of silent observation for a week
4. Reflection — what makes you happy vs what pleases you
5. Interview a family member about a core value
6. Family harmony — map the nine relationships in your life
7. Write about one relationship and how you might strengthen it
8. Observe how nature supports your daily life — a walk
9. List 10 things you use and trace them back to nature
10. Discuss a values-based dilemma in a small group
11. Case study — an engineer's ethical choice
12. Write a personal code of ethics
13. Choose one daily habit to change for a week and record it
14. Reflection paper — "where I stand today"
15. Commit to one value-driven action for the semester

## Assignments

1. Reflection journal — 5 entries over the semester
2. Self-exploration — "my desires and my needs"
3. Harmony in relationships — a written analysis
4. Nature and coexistence — an essay on sustainable living
5. Personal code of ethics for an engineer
